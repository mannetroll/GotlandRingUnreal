GotlandRingUnreal v0.1.0

The game, circuit assets and runtime DLLs are included. Unreal Editor is not required. Installation is per-user without administrator rights. A Start-menu shortcut and uninstaller are included; a desktop shortcut is optional.

Windows x64. Tested on Windows 11; Windows 10 has not been runtime-tested. A compatible graphics driver is required.
